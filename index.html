About Outera

Our mission

To empower global companies by connecting them with the elite talent of Latin America, providing a seamless, managed outbound infrastructure that drives growth without friction.

Our vision

To redefine the global workforce by proving that excellence has no borders, making Latin America the world's premier hub for high-impact sales talent.

Our story: born from Momentum, built for the future

Outera wasn't born in a boardroom; it was born in the middle of a massive transition.

When we learned that our previous home, Momentum, was being acquired by Salesforce, we faced a crossroads. We had spent years building a world-class team and a culture of excellence that felt like family. We weren't ready to let that go, and we weren't ready to see that talent diluted.

We decided to take a leap. We founded Outera to keep our team together and to prove a bold thesis: Argentinian talent isn't just "support", it's the engine.

We saw an opportunity to show US companies that the talent in Argentina doesn't just match US standards, it often exceeds them. We started Outera to bridge that gap and, more importantly, to give others the same life-changing opportunity we had: an entry point into the high-growth world of B2B SaaS.

For our clients, we are the 'Power Switch' for growth. For our team, we are the door to a world of global opportunity. We work together, we grow together, and we win together.

Our values

Borderless excellence

We believe talent is universal. We prove that LATAM professionals can out-perform and out-hustle any global standard.

Integrated partnership

We aren't a vendor; we are an extension of your team. Your growth is the only metric of our success.

Because we manage our team in-house, we provide consistency and transparency that "remote-only" models can't match.

The joy of the hustle

We believe productivity and happiness are linked. We cultivate an office environment where energy is high, and work is a source of pride.

Our manifesto

The world thinks 'cost-effective' means 'lower quality.' We're here to prove them wrong.

At Outera, we don't just hire people; we build careers for the brightest minds in Latin America. We believe that by creating an environment of happiness, high energy, and shared culture in our physical offices, we produce results that remote freelancers simply can't.

Work policies & logistics

Working hours

Working hours are 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (ART).

We work in a hybrid and flexible environment, but you're expected to be available during core working hours for meetings, collaboration, and communication.

Hybrid work model

Outera works under a 3 days in-office / 2 days optional WFH model.

When working remotely:

  • Stay active on Slack and email
  • Keep your calendar updated
  • Join meetings on time
  • Be available during working hours

PTO & vacation days

First 6 months:

  • You earn 1 vacation day per month worked

After 6 months:

  • You receive 15 vacation days per year

Requesting time off

  • Request PTO at least 30 days in advance
  • Coordinate dates with your manager/team
  • Update your calendar, Slack status, and Gmail auto-reply before your time off

Sick days

Sick days are separate from PTO.

If you're sick:

  • Notify your manager as early as possible
  • A medical certificate may be requested for extended absences

Office expectations

Please help maintain a professional and collaborative environment:

  • Keep shared spaces clean
  • Use headphones in open areas
  • Respect meeting rooms and shared spaces
  • Be mindful of noise levels

All-Hands meetings

We hold monthly All-Hands meetings to share company updates, priorities, wins, and challenges. Attendance is expected unless otherwise discussed with your manager.

SaaS & sales glossary

New to the SaaS world? Here's a quick guide to common terms and acronyms you'll hear every day.

For a comprehensive glossary with video explanations, check these resources:

📹 SaaS Glossary - Part 1 📹 SaaS Glossary - Part 2
CRM
Customer Relationship Management. A system for tracking customer interactions, deals, and communications (e.g. Salesforce, HubSpot).
ICP
Ideal Customer Profile. The description of the perfect customer for a company, industry, size, role, pain points.
Outbound
Proactive sales outreach to prospects who haven\'t yet contacted the company. Cold calling, cold emailing, LinkedIn outreach, etc.
Inbound
Leads that come to you. Prospects who fill out a form, request a demo, or reach out directly.
MQL
Marketing Qualified Lead. A prospect who matches the ideal customer profile and has shown interest in the product.
SQL
Sales Qualified Lead. An MQL that has been engaged by sales and is ready to have a conversation about a purchase.
BANT
Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline. Criteria to qualify a sales opportunity.
ARR
Annual Recurring Revenue. The yearly revenue generated from subscription-based customers.
CAC
Customer Acquisition Cost. The average cost to acquire one new customer.
LTV
Lifetime Value. The total revenue expected from a customer over their entire relationship with the company.
Churn
The percentage of customers who cancel their subscription in a given period.
SOW
Statement of Work. A document that outlines the scope, deliverables, and terms of a client engagement.
